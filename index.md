## ❓ FAQs

### What is an HLS link?

An HLS link is a URL that points to a type of media streaming. It is commonly used to deliver video and audio content over the internet.

### Can I use this app on my phone?

Currently, episodes is designed for desktop operating systems only (Windows, macOS, and Linux).
